# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes coffee shop retail sales data to identify sales
trends, customer behavior, product performance, store-level performance,
and opportunities for business improvement.

An interactive **Excel dashboard** was created using the sales data to
convert raw transactional data into meaningful business insights and
actionable recommendations.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data and
generate actionable insights that can help improve the overall
performance of the coffee shop.

The analysis focuses on:

-   Sales performance by day and hour
-   Peak sales periods
-   Monthly revenue trends
-   Store location performance
-   Average bill and order per person
-   Best-performing products
-   Category-wise sales performance

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Sales by Day and Hour

Sales activity is strongest during the morning period, particularly
between **8 AM and 10 AM**.

The dashboard identifies **10 AM** as the strongest hour, with
approximately **26,713 units ordered**.

### 2. Peak Sales Period

The major peak period occurs between **8 AM and 10 AM**.

This indicates that the coffee shop should focus staffing, inventory
availability, and service speed around the morning rush.

### 3. Monthly Sales

Monthly revenue increased significantly toward the later months of the
analysis period.

-   January: **\$81,677.74**
-   February: **\$76,145.19**
-   March: **\$98,834.68**
-   April: **\$118,941.08**
-   May: **\$156,727.76**
-   June: **\$166,485.88**

**June** recorded the highest monthly revenue.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1.  Morning hours, especially **8 AM--10 AM**, represent the strongest
    period of customer activity.
2.  **June** generated the highest monthly revenue.
3.  **Hell's Kitchen** is the strongest-performing store location.
4.  **Barista Espresso** is the top product by revenue.
5.  **Brewed Chai Tea** is the top product by quantity sold.
6.  **Coffee and Tea** are the dominant revenue-generating categories.
7.  The average bill of **\$4.69** provides an opportunity to increase
    customer spending through bundles and cross-selling.

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

### 1. Optimize Morning Operations

Increase staffing and ensure sufficient inventory during the **8 AM--10
AM** peak period.

### 2. Promote High-Performing Products

Prioritize products such as **Barista Espresso, Brewed Chai Tea, Hot
Chocolate, and Gourmet Brewed Coffee**.

### 3. Increase Average Order Value

Create combinations such as:

-   Coffee + Bakery
-   Tea + Snack
-   Coffee + Dessert

### 4. Benchmark Store Performance

Use **Hell's Kitchen** as a benchmark and analyze its operational and
customer-traffic patterns.

### 5. Focus on Core Categories

Continue prioritizing **Coffee and Tea**, while using lower-performing
categories for targeted promotions and cross-selling.

### 6. Investigate Revenue Growth

Analyze the factors responsible for the strong increase in revenue from
March through June and identify strategies that can be repeated.

## 📌 Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates that the coffee shop has strong demand during
morning hours, with Coffee and Tea serving as the primary revenue
drivers. The business can improve performance by optimizing peak-hour
operations, promoting high-performing products, increasing average order
value, and using location-level insights to improve customer traffic and
sales.
